What the numbers mean

PPI (pixels per inch)How many screen pixels fit in one inch. Higher means sharper text. Phones are usually 400 to 500, tablets 260 to 330, laptops 200 to 250. Phone makers and spec sheets often call this DPI too.
Page at % of print sizeHow big a PDF page looks on that screen compared with the same page printed on paper. At 80%, 12 pt text looks like 9.6 pt text.
Pixels per page inchHow many screen pixels each inch of the page gets. It's the effective resolution of the page on that screen, like a scanner's DPI. Around 150 or more looks crisp.
Sizes are screen onlyRectangles show the visible display area from the diagonal and pixel count, not bezels or the case. Diagonals are the maker's figures, which round a little and ignore rounded corners.
